# Content policy

These are the rules the pipeline enforces in code, and why. This is not legal advice. When in doubt, ask the rights holder.

## 1. Copyright

| Material | Rule |
|---|---|
| Rockstar gameplay, trailers, screenshots, artwork, in-game music/TV | **Not used.** Rockstar's "Policy on posting copyrighted Rockstar Games material" (last updated 10 Jan 2025) tolerates fan footage for **non-commercial** use, defined as not earning money from the footage. It says it is meant for *occasional* use by individual fans, and it doesn't cover trailers or artwork. It also bans pre-release leaked footage, story spoilers and isolated cutscenes. A daily, monetisable channel doesn't fit, so every visual is generated by code. Licensing questions go to copyright@take2games.com. |
| Other creators' videos, thumbnails, voices | Never used. The narrator persona is original and may not imitate, name or reference real streamers (enforced in the writer prompt). |
| Music | Only (a) the procedurally generated tracks this project creates, or (b) files you list in `assets/music/LICENSES.yaml` with a licence that covers YouTube **and** TikTok. Anything else is ignored. |
| Fonts | Poppins, SIL Open Font License (see `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`). |
| News sources | Facts are restated in our own words and linked in the description. Headlines and articles are never quoted at length. |

## 2. Facts and rumours

- Every claim must cite a fact in `data/facts.yaml` that has at least one source URL and a fresh `verified_on` date.
- `confirmed` is only for official Rockstar/Take-Two statements or platform data. `reported` is for reputable press or a company claim, and must be attributed ("Rockstar says…", "according to…"). `rumor` must be called a rumour in the narration, carries an orange **RUMOR** badge and is **never** in the title. `debunked` can only appear framed as a myth, followed by the correction.
- Numbers are checked literally: if "$69.99" isn't in the cited fact, the script is rejected.
- Time-sensitive facts expire, and stale facts are blocked until a human re-checks them.
- Descriptions list sources and state that RUMOR items are unconfirmed.

## 3. YouTube

- **Inauthentic content (YPP):** YouTube does not monetise "generic or repetitive" content that looks template-made or feels repetitive when watched in a row. The pipeline counters this with format rotation, style rotation, hook cooldowns, similarity checks, LLM-written scripts, a human review step (Stage 3) and a daily cap of 1–2 videos. Full automation (Stage 4) carries more of this risk than human-reviewed publishing. Your comments on review issues are the best defence.
- **AI disclosure:** YouTube requires the "altered or synthetic content" label for *realistic* content that could be mistaken for real people or events. Our narrator is a generic synthetic voice over clearly animated graphics, so `contains_synthetic_media` defaults to `false`. The description always says "Narration: AI-generated voice". If you add realistic AI imagery or clone a voice, set it to `true`.
- **Audience:** `selfDeclaredMadeForKids: false`. GTA is an M-rated game and the content isn't aimed at children. Narration stays clean (a profanity filter is in the verifier).
- **Spam:** no misleading titles, "free download" bait, sub-for-sub or engagement bait beyond normal questions. A banned-phrase list is enforced.

## 4. TikTok

- API posting requires an approved developer app. Until TikTok audits it, posts are private-only. The pipeline never works around this (no browser automation of the TikTok website).
- AI-generated content label: `is_aigc: true` by default.

## 5. Trademarks

"GTA", "Grand Theft Auto" and "Rockstar Games" are Take-Two trademarks. Don't name the channel or style it in a way that implies it's official. The description carries the "not affiliated" disclaimer, and the visuals deliberately avoid GTA logos and the Pricedown typeface.

## 6. Spoilers

After launch, avoid story spoilers in titles, hooks and thumbnails. The facts database only contains marketing-level information by default. If you add story facts, tag them `spoiler` and keep them out of titles.
